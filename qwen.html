About Amai Studio

Amai Studio is an artisanal factory of Japanese desserts located in Portugal. We draw inspiration from the tradition of Japanese daifuku and reinvent the concept with a European touch.

Our mochi gelato combines the soft exterior of mochi with the creamy interior of artisanal Italian gelato. Produced locally with selected ingredients, gluten-free, and with vegan and lactose-free options.

The perfect fusion between cultures, flavors, and lightness.
